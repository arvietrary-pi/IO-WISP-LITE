Estimate content

These prompts give your estimate package a consistent structure and an audit trail.

Recommended final package

Keep these deliverables together with the estimate.

  1. Document register / table of contents
  2. Scope summary and drawing/specification basis
  3. Detailed quantity takeoff or BOQ with sheet references
  4. Material, labor, equipment, and subcontractor pricing basis
  5. Assumptions, allowances, exclusions, alternates, and provisional sums
  6. Clarifications, RFIs, risks, constructability notes, and unresolved flags
  7. Revision/change log and final review record

Estimating roadmap

A preliminary sequence generated from the document set and standard estimating practice. Adjust it for the project before relying on it.

This is a planning aid, not an automatic construction schedule. The PDF can suggest drawing disciplines and review stages, but quantities, rates, duration, and final scope still require your judgment.
